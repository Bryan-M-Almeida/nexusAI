# Anatomy and the Human Body — Reference Values
categoria: Anatomy

Average adult values used in anatomy and physiology textbooks. There are no "exact" values: they vary
with sex, height, weight, age and fitness. Always give the average and the range.

## Heart
- Average weight: men about 280 to 340 g; women about 230 to 280 g. Roughly 0.4% to 0.5% of body weight.
- Size: about that of a closed fist, around 12 cm long.
- Four chambers: two atria and two ventricles. The right side pumps blood to the lungs; the left side, to the body.
- Resting heart rate (adults): 60 to 100 beats per minute. Trained athletes may be between 40 and 60.
- It beats about 100,000 times a day.

## Brain and nervous system
- Average brain weight: about 1.3 to 1.4 kg (men on average near 1.35 kg; women near 1.2 kg). Brain size does not indicate intelligence.
- About 86 billion neurons (estimate).
- Uses roughly 20% of the body's energy at rest.

## Lungs and breathing
- Right lung: 3 lobes, about 600 g. Left lung: 2 lobes, about 550 g (smaller to make room for the heart).
- Resting respiratory rate (adults): 12 to 20 breaths per minute.
- Normal oxygen saturation (pulse oximeter): 95% to 100%.

## Liver, kidneys and other organs
- Liver: largest internal organ, about 1.4 to 1.6 kg.
- Kidneys: two, each about 120 to 170 g and 10 to 12 cm long. They filter about 180 liters of plasma a day, producing 1 to 2 liters of urine.
- Spleen: about 150 g (common range 100 to 250 g).
- Skin: the body's largest organ, with an area of about 1.5 to 2 m².
- Small intestine: about 6 to 7 m. Large intestine: about 1.5 m.

## Blood and body fluids
- Blood volume: about 5 liters in adults (roughly 7% of body weight); men about 5 to 6 L, women about 4 to 5 L.
- Body water: about 50% to 60% of body weight (higher in men, on average).
- ABO blood groups: A, B, AB and O, with Rh positive or negative. O- is the universal red-cell donor; AB+ is the universal recipient.

## Bones, muscles and teeth
- Adults: 206 bones. Newborns have about 270 to 300 bony parts, which fuse as they grow.
- Largest bone: femur. Smallest bone: stapes, in the middle ear.
- More than 600 skeletal muscles.
- Teeth: 20 baby (deciduous) teeth and 32 permanent teeth (including 4 wisdom teeth).

## Reference vital signs (adult at rest)
- Blood pressure: ideally below 120/80 mmHg. From 130/80 mmHg on repeated readings, seek medical evaluation (US guideline; other countries use 140/90).
- Body temperature: about 36.1 °C to 37.2 °C (97 °F to 99 °F). Fever: from 38 °C (100.4 °F).
- Heart rate: 60 to 100 bpm. Respiratory rate: 12 to 20 per minute. Saturation: 95% to 100%.
- Fasting blood glucose: normal below 100 mg/dL; 100 to 125 indicates prediabetes; 126 or more (confirmed) indicates diabetes.
- BMI (body mass index) = weight (kg) ÷ height² (m). WHO normal range: 18.5 to 24.9.

## Numbers and curiosities
- The adult human body has about 30 to 37 trillion cells (estimates).
- Eyes blink about 15 to 20 times a minute.
- Hair grows about 1 cm a month; fingernails about 3 mm a month.
