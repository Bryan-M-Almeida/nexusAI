# Health and Well-being
categoria: Health

General guidance based on public health recommendations (such as the WHO). Not a substitute for medical advice.

## Sleep
- Adults: 7 to 9 hours a night. Teenagers: 8 to 10 hours.
- Keep regular sleep and wake times, weekends included.
- Avoid screens and bright light for 1 hour before bed; keep the bedroom dark, quiet and cool.
- Avoid caffeine in the 6 hours before bed and heavy meals at night.
- Short naps (up to 20 minutes) in the early afternoon don't hurt night sleep.
- Loud snoring with breathing pauses and daytime sleepiness may indicate sleep apnea: see a doctor.

## Physical activity
WHO recommendation for adults:
- 150 to 300 minutes a week of moderate activity (brisk walking, easy cycling) or 75 to 150 minutes of vigorous activity (running).
- Muscle strengthening at least twice a week.
- Sit less: get up and move every 30 to 60 minutes.
Any movement is better than none. Start slowly and progress gradually. People with chronic conditions should talk to a doctor before starting intense exercise.

## Nutrition
- Prefer whole or minimally processed foods.
- Fill half your plate with vegetables; include protein (beans, eggs, meat, fish) and mostly whole-grain carbohydrates.
- Limit ultra-processed foods, added sugar, salt (under 5 g a day, about 1 teaspoon) and sugary drinks.
- Eat mindfully, away from screens, and respect hunger and fullness signals.

## Hydration
A common reference is about 2 to 3 liters of fluids a day for adults, including water from food — needs vary with climate, body size and activity. Pale urine indicates good hydration. Drink more in hot weather and during exercise. Older adults feel less thirst and should drink regularly.

## Mental health
- Warning signs: persistent sadness for more than 2 weeks, loss of interest, sleep or appetite changes, isolation, thoughts of death.
- Seek help: a doctor, psychologist or local mental health service.
- Crisis lines: USA 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, call or text), UK Samaritans 116 123, Brazil CVV 188, Spain 024.
- Immediate danger: call emergency services (911 / 112).
- Helpful practices: regular sleep, exercise, social contact, time in nature, limiting news and social media, breathing techniques.

## Breathing and relaxation techniques
- Box breathing: inhale for 4 seconds, hold 4, exhale 4, hold 4. Repeat for 2 to 5 minutes.
- 4-7-8 breathing: inhale through the nose for 4, hold for 7, exhale through the mouth for 8. Helps you wind down before sleep.
- Physiological sigh: two short inhales through the nose followed by one long exhale through the mouth — calms quickly.
- 5-4-3-2-1 grounding for anxiety: name 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell and 1 you taste.

## Ergonomics and computer work
- Top of the monitor at eye level, about an arm's length away.
- Feet flat on the floor, knees and elbows near 90 degrees, back supported.
- 20-20-20 rule for your eyes: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet (6 meters) away for 20 seconds.
- Take active breaks and stretch your neck, shoulders, wrists and back.

## Prevention and check-ups
- Keep vaccinations up to date.
- Check blood pressure periodically; ideal is below 120/80 mmHg. Repeated readings of 130/80 or higher deserve medical evaluation.
- Routine tests according to age and medical advice (blood sugar, cholesterol, cancer screenings).
- Wear sunscreen daily and avoid strong midday sun.
- Don't smoke; if you drink alcohol, keep it moderate.
